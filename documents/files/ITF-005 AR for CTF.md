# Augmented Reality for the Capture the Flag

## AR-Powered Capture The Flag (CTF) Application – Bsides Limburg
An interactive mobile application is being developed for Bsides Limburg, bringing the traditional Capture The Flag (CTF) concept into the physical world through Augmented Reality (AR).

The project combines hands-on cybersecurity challenges and gamification with physical activity across the campus. Coaches can strategically deploy physical checkpoints and virtual markers, which participants then locate using the mobile application.
## Objectives & Scope
- Transform traditional CTF challenges into an active, real-world (IRL) experience.

- Encourage participants to explore the campus, collaborate and develop their problem-solving skills.

- Provide coaches with intuitive tools to manage checkpoints and challenges on location, and monitor participant progress in real time.
## Core Features & User Roles


| **User Role** | **Functionality** |
| --- | --- |
| **Coach / Administrator** | Place and manage AR markers and physical checkpoints across the campus. Associate specific codes/flags, hints and point values with individual challenges. Monitor scoreboards and flag validation status for individual participants or teams in real time. |
| **Student / Participant** | Use the AR camera view to scan their physical surroundings and locate hidden flags and challenges. |
## Technical Architecture & Technology Stack
- Mobile Application

  - Developed entirely in Flutter, providing a consistent cross-platform experience on iOS and Android from a single codebase.

  - Integration of AR plugins to support spatial tracking, camera overlays and interactive checkpoint detection.

- Backend & API

  - REST- or WebSocket-based API to enable real-time synchronisation of scores and checkpoint data.

- Database & Storage

  - Storage and management of user roles, campus coordinates and AR marker data.
## Value & Expected Outcomes
An interactive and competitive way for students to gain hands-on experience with CTF concepts and cybersecurity challenges. The Flutter-based architecture makes the application easy to extend, maintain and redeploy for future editions of Bsides Limburg or other training events.
